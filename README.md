### SATCEN – Project Description

**SATCEN (Surgical Asset Trading & Compliance Exchange Network)** is a smart web-based platform designed to help hospitals and healthcare organizations **list, search, buy, rent, allocate, and manage surgical equipment and medical assets** efficiently.

The system provides **role-based access** for authenticated hospitals and administrators. It includes an **AI/ML-based pricing engine** that estimates the appropriate price of surgical equipment based on factors such as equipment age, usage, maintenance history, brand, depreciation, and market demand. An **AI chatbot** assists users with asset-related queries, while a **priority/ recommendation system** helps hospitals identify suitable equipment based on urgency, availability, and requirements.

The platform also supports **asset tracking, compliance management, transaction management, and real-time availability**, improving resource utilization and reducing delays caused by equipment shortages.

**Technologies:** React.js, Python, Flask/Django, MySQL, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Machine Learning.
